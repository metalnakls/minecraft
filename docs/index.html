Minecraft for your Mac

Apple Silicon · macOS 15 or later

Paste this in Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://metalnakls.github.io/minecraft/install.sh | sh

Includes the native launcher, Java, Minecraft, mods and current settings. MCUI and Metal Shaders check for compatible updates before each game launch, enabled by default. Downloads are verified and replaced mods are backed up. Saves and accounts are never copied.

The launcher is ad-hoc signed. macOS may require Open Anyway. After installation, open Minecraft in your user Applications folder and add your own player.

Check without installing: curl -fsSL https://metalnakls.github.io/minecraft/install.sh | sh -s -- --dry-run
